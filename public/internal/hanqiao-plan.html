汉桥项目
落地方案HanQiao · Delivery Plan for a Three-country Pilot · v3.0

本方案旨在说明汉桥（AI 中文学习平台）在三个国家、各一所学校的落地路径，并以试点数据作为下一阶段对外合作的依据。全文按「对外洽谈 → 需求调研 → 技术验证 → 落地实施」四条工作流展开，附应用场景、技术路线、备忘录与合同参考样本。时间窗口只写月份与所需时长；所有签约方在正式签署前均为「待定」；凡属待验证的假设，均在文中单独标注。

00 · Executive summary

执行摘要One page

本项目的核心任务是将远程中文老师、可编排的智能体与离线优先的学习端纳入三所学校的常规课表，在弱网、停电与共享设备条件下完成六周教学，并取得可核证的运行数据。

项目一句话

以老师为根，以三国三校为试金石，以弱网和离线为默认，把合规埋进地基里。对外入口为 learn.gacee.org，证书验真为 verify.gacee.org。

目标

9 月完成立项、候选校筛选与技术验证启动；10 月底冻结 MVP；11 月起三国各一所学校开课六周；年底前完成复盘并决定扩大范围。

试点格局

三个国家 × 各一所学校 × 每校 20–40 名学员。三国分别覆盖英语、斯瓦希里语、法语三种辅助语言与三套数据法规，用同一套系统验证「可迁移」。

落地角色

协会（内容、师资、国际资源、项目设计）+ 试点校（学员组织与现场）+ 远程中文老师（教学与签发）+ 技术平台（系统与 AI）。签约主体在各国法务确认后确定。

八条原则

老师主导、无幻觉、普惠优先、弱网离线默认、母语脚手架、合规入地基、先轻后重、对齐标准与当地体系。

前置判断

在两个月内使真实班级投入使用，前提是将 MVP 界定为「录播 + 现成会议工具直播 + 可安装网页应用（PWA）离线练习 + 伴学与复习两个智能体」。原生离线安卓 App 需要 10–12 周成熟期，安排在试点开课后启动，于阶段 2 发布测试版。此安排并非缩减范围，而是先在网页端实现「弱网离线」原则，再迁移至原生端。

成功标准（试点复盘时逐条验收）

  1. 三国各一所学校、每校 20–40 名学员完成 6 周学习，出勤率（含回放）与作业提交率有系统记录。
  2. 老师每周实际投入课时下降，学员练习量上升，两者都有日志数据；每老师课时服务的学员数有实测倍数。
  3. 智能体答疑的抽样准确率与「置信不足即转人工」的比例被度量，没有一起未被发现的错误判定。
  4. 断网与停电条件下学员仍能完成练习并在联网后同步，零数据丢失；三国网络实测数据可比。
  5. 学校数据隔离、监护人同意、跨境传输三项合规要求在三国各有书面证据。
  6. 产出「每学员每月成本」实测值，成为之后与教育部门、企业与基金会谈价格的唯一依据。

02 · Three-country pilot

三国对照设计

三校使用同一套内容（HSK 一级前 6 周）与同一套系统，只有三个变量不同：辅助语言、网络画像、法规。复盘时按这三个变量做横向对照，得到「哪些能力是可迁移的，哪些必须按国家定制」的结论，这是阶段 3 规模化的依据。

06 · Architecture & technical route

技术架构与技术路线JE Engine, the Agent Harness, and TIDAR KAG

汉桥的技术基座为 JurisEdu 已投入生产运行的 JE 引擎、Agent Harness 与 TIDAR KAG。本项目在该基座之上新增三层能力：跨境弱网媒体层（直播 / 录播 / 内容包）、离线优先客户端（PWA / App）以及按学员个性化记忆的智能体编排层。本章说明各层的组件规格、接口约定与验收指标；凡涉及时延、带宽、准确率的数值，均为需在阶段 0 实测的工作假设。

架构原则

云中立（Kubernetes 声明式部署、S3 兼容存储、OpenTelemetry 观测、Model Gateway 抽象模型供应商）；数据驻留新加坡；智能体只提议不执行写操作；所有学习产物挂追加式哈希链证据；任一网络段退化均有确定的降级路径。

一、地理拓扑：三段网络路径

中国 · 老师接入段

  • 老师端（Web / 桌面）：直播推流、板书批注、复核队列、学情看板
  • 跨境接入：会议工具的中国大陆节点或全球加速；平台仅传信令与业务数据，不在中国侧部署媒体节点（ADR-6）
  • 班主任智能体（阶段 2）：排课、预警、沟通草拟

核心区域 · Agent Harness（新加坡）

  • 客户端网关：Ingress、OIDC 认证、按租户限流、媒体信令
  • 媒体层：会议工具适配 / RTC 适配、录制回收、转码、VOD 源站
  • 领域服务：课程、排课、评测、学员、证书、收款登记
  • JE 引擎 · JE Agent 混合编排：Router → 专才 Agent → 工具 → Verifier → 转人工
  • 知识与记忆：中文课程图谱、掌握度、四层记忆
  • 数据 / 存储、观测、安全、合规、审计留痕

海外 · 学员接入段（三国试点校）

  • 边缘 CDN / 学校缓存点：录播与内容包就近分发
  • 学员端 PWA（离线优先）：本地库、内容包、端侧练习与判分
  • 专属智能体花名册：伴学、复习先上；发音、对话后置
  • 共享设备多账号：每账号独立加密本地存储

实时与 API 流量走加速链路；录播与内容分发走 CDN；三段路径各自优化，任一段退化均触发本章第六节的降级路径。

5 回答或转人工有出处的个性化回答；置信不足、主观题、写动作一律进入老师队列弃权门 + propose → approve—
6 写记忆会话摘要写情节记忆；作答事件更新掌握度；轨迹入库R3 + R5异步

四、TIDAR KAG：低幻觉的来源

TIDAR KAG 为平台自研的「知识图谱可溯源生成」，区别于通用向量 RAG。先在法律域验证，再移植到教育课程图谱并已上线。三个支柱构成其不编造的技术保证。

时序 Temporal

任何判断均基于某一时间点之前的真实作答快照（as-of）；两个时间点之间可做逐知识点差分：进步、回落、新评估、无变化，如实呈现。

可溯源 Provenance

每个结论挂真实证据：作答记录、错题干、课程图谱中的先修 / 相关邻域；回答带出处，无出处则不答。

可校准 Calibrated

掌握等级不用点估计，而用 Wilson 置信区间下界（z = 1.96）判定；作答少于 3 题一律「证据不足」弃权，0 题为「尚未评估」。

六、跨境弱网直播、录播分发与离线客户端

直播选型要点取舍
A 现成会议工具Zoom / 腾讯会议承载直播；平台通过深链集成，负责排课、点名、录制回收、课堂事件回流；按学员所在地区推荐入口成本最低、落地最快；课堂定制受限。MVP 采用
B 商用 RTC SDK需自定义课堂 / 白板 / 深度嵌入时，采用具全球实时网络的 SDK（阿里云 RTC / Agora / 腾讯 RTC / ZEGO）体验可控；按分钟计费。阶段 2 按 PoC-1 结论评估
C 自建 SFUWebRTC SFU（LiveKit / mediasoup）+ 自建 TURN + 跨境加速边际成本低但运维重；规模化后评估

三级降级与录制兜底

  • 一级：全互动直播（音视频 + 白板 + 实时问答）
  • 二级：关闭摄像头，保留音频 + 板书 / 幻灯 + 字幕 + 文字互动
  • 三级：网络不可用时转「课后 15 分钟内可回看的录播 + 智能体陪练补齐」
  • 每堂直播默认云端录制转 VOD；掉线学员计入「回放出勤」；降级事件回流到课堂事件日志

弱网工程清单

  • 自适应码率 + Simulcast / SVC，分辨率帧率自动降级，优先保音频与白板
  • Opus + DTX + FEC / NACK / PLC 抗丢包；自适应抖动缓冲
  • TURN 中继兜底 NAT 穿透失败
  • 音频优先模式：只留语音 + 板书 + 字幕

录播与内容分发

  • 老师录课或直播录制 → 源站对象存储；自动字幕（中 / 英 / 法），阶段 2 加老师音色旁白
  • FFmpeg 多码率 HLS 阶梯：240p 300 kbps · 360p 600 kbps · 480p 1 Mbps · 720p 2 Mbps · 纯音频 48 kbps；6 秒切片；AES-128 分包密钥
  • 源站 → 多 CDN，非洲边缘就近取流，断点续传；学校缓存点为常开设备上的反向缓存
  • 内容包：按节 / 单元打包（清单 + 版本号 + 校验和），可限 Wi-Fi 下载，退课可回收

离线优先客户端（PWA）

  • 本地可用：已下载录播、随堂练、复习卡、笔顺、客观题作答与判分、录音留存
  • 本地库（IndexedDB），内容包版本化；出站事件队列，幂等键；失败重试与指数退避；断点续传
  • 同步协议：事件溯源，服务器单调序号为权威；学习进度「服务器权威 + 事件合并」，笔记 last-write-wins
  • 存储预算 ≤ 150 MB，分级清理；省流模式；共享设备多账号，每账号以 PIN 派生密钥（Argon2id）加密本地库

二、合同管理流程

  1. 模板：本章样本为统一模板；协会法务定稿英文主文。
  2. 本地化：每国一位当地律师审校数据保护、未成年人、劳务与税务条款，输出「本地化差异表」。
  3. 签约方确认：由 Ray 确认学校法人主体与授权签字人；由协会负责人确认协会签字人。
  4. 签署：电子签为主（DocuSign 或同等工具）；当地要求纸质时以扫描件为准并留存原件。
  5. 台账：项目经理维护合同台账（文书、版本、签署日、期限、到期提醒、数据附件版本）。
  6. 变更：范围、期限、数据用途的变更以补充协议形式签署，并同步更新合规登记册。

This plan sets out how HanQiao, the Association's AI-enabled Chinese-learning platform, is to be delivered in three countries with one school in each, and how the pilot data will serve as the basis for the next round of external partnerships. It follows four parallel workstreams — external engagement, needs research, technical validation and delivery — and includes application scenarios, the technical route, and reference samples for memoranda and contracts. Time windows are expressed in months and required durations only; every signatory is marked “to be confirmed” until execution; every unverified assumption is flagged in the text.

00 · Executive summary

Executive summary执行摘要

The core task is to place remote Chinese teachers, orchestrable agents and an offline-first learner client into the regular timetables of three schools, complete six weeks of teaching under weak-network, power-cut and shared-device conditions, and obtain verifiable operating data.

The project in one sentence

Teachers first; three countries and three schools as the proving ground; weak network and offline by default; compliance in the foundation. The public entry point is learn.gacee.org and certificate verification is verify.gacee.org.

Objectives

Approve the project, shortlist schools and start technical validation in September; freeze the MVP by the end of October; open six-week classes in one school per country from November; complete the review and decide on expansion before year end.

Pilot footprint

Three countries × one school each × 20–40 learners per school. The three countries cover English, Kiswahili and French as scaffolding languages and three data-protection regimes, validating portability on one system.

Delivery roles

The Association (content, teachers, international resources, programme design) + pilot schools (learner organisation and on-site delivery) + remote Chinese teachers (teaching and sign-off) + the technology platform (systems and AI). Signatories are confirmed after legal review in each country.

Eight principles

Teacher-led; no hallucination; inclusion first; weak-network and offline by default; mother-tongue scaffolding; compliance in the foundation; light before heavy; aligned with standards and local systems.

Prior judgement

Bringing a real class into use within two months is feasible only if the MVP is defined as recorded lessons + live classes on existing meeting tools + offline practice in an installable web app (PWA) + two agents (study companion and review). A native offline Android app needs 10–12 weeks to mature; it starts after the pilot opens and ships as a beta in Phase 2. This is not a reduction in scope: the weak-network-and-offline principle is delivered on the web first and migrated to native afterwards.

Success criteria (accepted item by item at the pilot review)

  1. One school per country, 20–40 learners each, completes six weeks; attendance (including replays) and homework submission are recorded by the system.
  2. Teachers' weekly hours fall while learner practice rises, both evidenced by logs; the number of learners served per teacher hour is measured.
  3. Sampled accuracy of agent answers and the hand-off rate are measured; no incorrect judgement goes undetected.
  4. Learners complete practice offline during outages and sync afterwards with zero data loss; network measurements are comparable across the three countries.
  5. School data isolation, guardian consent and cross-border transfer are each evidenced in writing in all three countries.
  6. A measured cost per learner per month is produced and becomes the sole basis for pricing discussions with education authorities, employers and foundations.

06 · Architecture & technical route

Architecture and technical route技术架构与技术路线

HanQiao's technical base is the JE Engine, the Agent Harness and TIDAR KAG already in production at JurisEdu. This project adds three layers: cross-border weak-network media (live / recordings / content packs), an offline-first client (PWA / app), and per-learner personalised agent orchestration. This chapter specifies component design, interface conventions and acceptance metrics; every latency, bandwidth and accuracy figure is a working assumption to be measured in Phase 0.

Architecture principles

Cloud-neutral (declarative Kubernetes deployment, S3-compatible storage, OpenTelemetry observability, Model Gateway abstracting model vendors); data resident in Singapore; agents propose and never execute writes; every learning artefact carries append-only hash-chain evidence; every network segment has a defined degradation path.

1. Geographic topology: three network segments

China · teacher access

  • Teacher client (web / desktop): live publishing, board annotation, review queue, class insight
  • Cross-border access via the meeting tool's mainland nodes or global acceleration; the platform carries only signalling and business data and deploys no media node in China (ADR-6)
  • Homeroom agent (Phase 2): scheduling, alerts, drafted communication

Core region · Agent Harness (Singapore)

  • Client gateway: ingress, OIDC authentication, per-tenant rate limits, media signalling
  • Media layer: meeting-tool / RTC adapter, recording retrieval, transcoding, VOD origin
  • Domain services: courses, scheduling, assessment, learners, certificates, collection ledger
  • JE Engine · JE Agent hybrid orchestration: Router → specialist agent → tools → Verifier → hand-off
  • Knowledge and memory: Chinese course graph, mastery, four-layer memory
  • Data / storage, observability, security, compliance, audit trail

Overseas · learner access (pilot schools)

  • Edge CDN / school cache point: recordings and packs served nearby
  • Learner PWA (offline-first): local store, packs, on-device practice and marking
  • Personal agent roster: companion and review first; pronunciation and conversation later
  • Shared-device multi-account: separately encrypted local store per account

Real-time and API traffic use the accelerated path; recordings and packs use the CDN; each segment is optimised separately, and degradation of any segment triggers the paths in section 6 of this chapter.

4. TIDAR KAG: the source of low hallucination

TIDAR KAG is the platform's own knowledge-graph, provenance-bound generation, distinct from generic vector RAG. It was validated in the legal domain first, then ported to the educational course graph and is in production. Three pillars provide the technical guarantee against fabrication.

Temporal

Every judgement rests on a real answer snapshot as of a point in time; two points can be diffed per knowledge point — progress, regression, newly assessed, unchanged — and reported as such.

Provenance

Every conclusion is attached to real evidence: answer records, missed items, prerequisite and related neighbourhoods in the course graph; answers carry sources, and without a source there is no answer.

Calibrated

Mastery is not a point estimate but the lower bound of a Wilson confidence interval (z = 1.96); fewer than three attempts is “insufficient evidence” and the agent abstains; zero attempts is “not yet assessed”.

Three-level degradation and recording fallback

  • Level 1: fully interactive live (audio-video + whiteboard + live Q&A)
  • Level 2: camera off; audio + board / slides + captions + text chat retained
  • Level 3: network unavailable → recording available within 15 minutes + agent-led practice to fill the gap
  • Every live class is cloud-recorded to VOD by default; dropped learners count as replay attendance; degradation events flow to the classroom event log

Weak-network engineering checklist

  • Adaptive bitrate + simulcast / SVC; resolution and frame rate degrade automatically; audio and whiteboard prioritised
  • Opus + DTX + FEC / NACK / PLC against loss; adaptive jitter buffer
  • TURN relay when NAT traversal fails
  • Audio-first mode: voice + board + captions only

Recordings and content delivery

  • Teacher recording or live capture → origin object storage; automatic subtitles (Chinese / English / French); teacher-voice narration in Phase 2
  • FFmpeg HLS ladder: 240p 300 kbps · 360p 600 kbps · 480p 1 Mbps · 720p 2 Mbps · audio-only 48 kbps; 6-second segments; AES-128 per-pack keys
  • Origin → multi-CDN; African edges serve nearby with resumable download; the school cache point is a reverse cache on an always-on device
  • Content packs per lesson / unit (manifest + version + checksum); Wi-Fi-only download option; revocable on withdrawal

Offline-first client (PWA)

  • Available locally: downloaded recordings, in-class practice, review cards, stroke order, objective answering and marking, recorded audio
  • Local store (IndexedDB), versioned packs; outbound event queue with idempotency keys; retry with exponential back-off; resumable transfers
  • Sync protocol: event-sourced, server monotonic sequence as authority; progress = server authority + event merge; notes = last-write-wins
  • Storage budget ≤ 150 MB with tiered clean-up; data-saver mode; shared-device multi-account with per-account local encryption keyed from the PIN (Argon2id)

Lead teacher, local facilitator, learners, operations on duty

S3 · Offline practice during a power cut and next-day sync

Trigger
Power-cut window in the profile memory (e.g. 19:00–21:00); the Companion packs the day's tasks for offline use and moves the reminder to 17:30

Learner completes review cards and watches the recording offline → events queued → synced in sequence next day → mastery updated → teacher client shows “completed offline”

PoC-4 criteria; the teacher client distinguishes online from offline completion
Exceptions

Steps

Two-agent agreement rate recorded; the teacher sees only disagreements and subjective items; zero undetected errors
Exceptions
Blurred photo → prompt to retake with an example; low recognition confidence → whole submission to the teacher

Steps

Sourced rate 100%; handed-off questions closed with a record; 50 items reviewed by a person weekly

Steps

2. Contract management process

  1. Templates: the samples here are the common templates; the Association's counsel finalises the English master text.
  2. Localisation: one local lawyer per country reviews data-protection, minors, labour and tax clauses and produces a localisation variance table.
  3. Signatory confirmation: Ray confirms the school's legal entity and authorised signatory; the Association lead confirms the Association's signatory.
  4. Execution: electronic signature by default (DocuSign or equivalent); where paper is required locally, scans govern and originals are retained.
  5. Register: the programme manager maintains the contract register (instrument, version, signing date, term, renewal reminder, data-schedule version).
  6. Changes: changes to scope, term or data purposes are made by supplementary agreement and mirrored in the compliance register.

3. Key clauses and reference samples

C2 · Pilot MOU (structure and sample clauses)

1 Purpose and scope
A six-week Chinese-learning pilot for 20–40 learners at [School]; content is the first six weeks of HSK Level 1; delivered through the learn.gacee.org platform.
2 Term
From signature until completion of the pilot review; either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice; termination does not affect Clauses 7, 8 and 10.
3 Association obligations
Provide course content, remote teachers, platform access, training and support; charge no fee during the pilot; issue learning reports and completion certificates.
4 School obligations
Organise learners, provide a fixed timetable and venue, appoint a facilitator, assist with consent collection, permit on-site network measurement.
5 Fees
Free during the pilot; the School bears no system cost; the Association charges learners and guardians nothing.
6 Intellectual property
Course content, materials and brand belong to the Association; learner work belongs to the learner and may be used by the Association for teaching and non-commercial display with consent; platform software is owned by the Association's technology service provider and licensed to the School for the pilot.
7 Data protection
Per Schedule C3; the Association is controller, the School joint controller of its own learners' data; cross-border transfer only with guardian consent and in compliance with local law.
8 Confidentiality
Both parties keep unpublished pilot data, content and commercial information confidential for two years after termination.
9 Publicity
Prior written consent for any public reference to the pilot; separate consent for learner images and work.
10 Liability and disputes
Except Clauses 6–9, this MOU creates no binding obligations; disputes go to negotiation, then [SIAC / local court, to be fixed by counsel].
11 After the pilot
The parties may enter a formal agreement after the review; the School may request export or deletion of its learners' data.
Sample clause · Purpose and non-binding nature

This Memorandum of Understanding records the intention of the Global Association of Cultural and Educational Exchange (“the Association”) and [School] (“the School”) to conduct a six-week Chinese-language learning pilot for 20 to 40 learners using the Association's learning platform. Except for Clauses 6 (Intellectual Property), 7 (Data Protection), 8 (Confidentiality) and 9 (Publicity), this Memorandum does not create legally binding obligations. No fee is payable by the School, the learners or their guardians during the pilot.

10 · Pedagogy & content

Pedagogy and content教研与内容

Course content cannot be generated by technology in place of curriculum work. Curriculum is the effort most likely to become the bottleneck and must start in parallel with engineering. All three pilots use the same content; only the scaffolding language differs.

  • Standards alignment: anchored to HSK 3.0 (three bands, nine levels) and YCT; every knowledge point carries level and source in the graph; platform certificates are strictly separate from official certification.
  • Production method: teachers write the syllabus and key explanations; AI drafts exercises and variants; teachers review item by item before entry; unreviewed content never enters the graph.
  • Publishing and Future Academy: modules coded to the Academy's four strands; strong lecture notes and learner work become publishing material; competition prompts serve as writing practice; new titles are listed in the library automatically.
  • Local systems: the pilot collects the three countries' curriculum standards and term rhythms in preparation for certification discussions in Phase 3.

14 · Appendix

Appendix · Templates附录 · 模板

A. Principal interview guide (60 minutes)

  1. School profile: grades, enrolment, current Chinese provision, weekly hours and slots.
  2. Devices and network: number and condition of school devices, operator and tariffs, power-cut frequency, shared classrooms and projector, room for a cache-point device.
  3. Teachers and facilitators: who organises the class; share of mother tongue versus English / French / Kiswahili.
  4. Parents: channels, payment habits and capacity, attitude to children's data.
  5. Outcomes: what parents and the school value most (certificate, exam, speaking); awareness of HSK / YCT.
  6. Concerns: about AI, about data, about time and cost.
  7. Cooperation: what the school will provide, what it expects from the Association, expectations of the free pilot and afterwards.

B. PoC record fields

Card number · country and school · date and time slot · location and operator · device · executor · steps · raw measurements · pass / fail · evidence files (screenshots / logs / audio) · conclusions and recommendations · reviewer.

C. Pilot weekly report template (one page, per school)

This week's numbers (active, attendance incl. replay, practice, homework, accuracy, hand-off, cost) · three things that went well · three issues and owners · next week's plan · decisions needed from the board or the school.

D. School scoring sheet

Country · school · contact · network and power (1–5) · demand and organisation (1–5) · partner maturity (1–5) · compliance feasibility (1–5) · total · visit date · conclusion (sign / alternate / exclude).

E. Contract register fields

Instrument ID · type · parties · country · version · signing date · effective date · expiry · data-schedule version · localisation variance table · storage location · renewal reminder.
